# SRD Feature Hook Analysis

This report analyzes 407 unique features from the Daggerheart SRD to determine if the lifecycle hooks (`onIntent`, `onReviewAction`, `onReviewOutcome`, `onResolve`, plus Chips and Declarative APIs) are sufficient to implement them.

## Summary

- **Passive / Declarative (12)**: Handled by `passiveStatMods` or `virtualWeapons`.
- **Character Creation (4)**: Handled by Chips with `placements: ['create']` or declarative stats.
- **Advantage Triggers (18)**: Handled by `advantageTriggers` with `when()` conditions.
- **Action / Narrative (164)**: Handled by Chips with `placements: ['card']`.
- **Roll Modifiers (105)**: Handled by `onIntent` (modifying dice/stats before the roll).
- **Effect Interceptors (15)**: Handled by `onReviewOutcome` (modifying pending effects or rerolling).
- **Post-Roll Effects (14)**: Handled by `onResolve` (reacting to applied effects).
- **Rest / Session (8)**: Handled by `onIntent`/`onResolve` checking for rest/session action types.
- **Complex / Uncategorized (67)**: May require a combination of Chips and Hooks, or custom state.

**Conclusion**: The Action Loop architecture (`onIntent`, `onReviewAction`, `onReviewOutcome`, `onResolve`) combined with the Chip system and Declarative API is highly robust and appears sufficient to implement the vast majority of SRD features. The "Complex" category mostly contains narrative abilities or features that require a combination of a Chip (for player choice) and a Hook (for the mechanical effect).

### Passive / Declarative (12)
Features that statically modify stats or grant virtual weapons. Implemented via `passiveStatMods` or `virtualWeapons` without hooks.

- **Protective** (weapons - Labrys Axe, weapons - Round Shield): +1 to Armor Score
- **Barrier** (weapons - Tower Shield): +2 to Armor Score; -1 to Evasion
- **Protective** (weapons - Improved Round Shield): +2 to Armor Score
- **Barrier** (weapons - Improved Tower Shield): +3 to Armor Score; -1 to Evasion
- **Double Duty** (weapons - Spiked Shield): +1 to Armor Score; +1 to primary weapon damage within Melee range
- **Protective** (weapons - Advanced Round Shield): +3 to Armor Score
- **Barrier** (weapons - Advanced Tower Shield): +4 to Armor Score; -1 to Evasion
- **Protective** (weapons - Legendary Round Shield): +4 to Armor Score
- **Barrier** (weapons - Legendary Tower Shield): +5 to Armor Score; -1 to Evasion.
- **Flexible** (armor - Gambeson Armor, armor - Improved Gambeson Armor, armor - Advanced Gambeson Armor, armor - Legendary Gambeson Armor): +1 to Evasion
- **Codex-Touched** (abilities - Codex-Touched): When 4 or more of the domain cards in your loadout are from the Codex domain, gain the following benefits:  - You can **mark a Stress** to add your Proficiency to a Spellcast Roll. - Once per rest, replace this card with any card from your vault without paying its Recall Cost.
- **Bold Presence** (abilities - Bold Presence): When you make a Presence Roll, you can **spend a Hope** to add your Strength to the roll.  Additionally, once per rest when you would gain a condition, you can describe how your bold presence aids you in the situation and avoid gaining the condition.

### Character Creation (4)
Features that require choices during character creation. Implemented via Chips with `placements: ['create']`.

- **Purposeful Design** (ancestries - Clank): Decide who made you and for what purpose. At character creation, choose one of your Experiences that best aligns with this purpose and gain a permanent +1 bonus to it.
